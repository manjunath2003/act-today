How the score works (and change the weights)

Score = (w₁·Trigger + w₂·Fit + w₃·Budget + w₄·Reach) × (0.75 + 0.25·Confidence)

Trigger fades with age: 0.4 + 0.6 × 0.5^(days/14). A week-old event keeps ~82% of its force, a month-old one ~46%.

Confidence comes from the sources, not from a feeling: independent origins, whether one is a primary source, and unresolved conflicts. Ten outlets repeating one press release count once.

Action rule: strong trigger + confidence ≥ 0.6 + fit ≥ 45 → Act today. Strong trigger but thin data → Verify first. Strong trigger but fit < 45 → Low fit.

How this stays fresh without me

The data above was researched by hand and by AI on 29 Sep 2026. The companion repo turns the same steps into a pipeline that runs on a schedule and writes to a database, so the list rebuilds itself every morning.

  1. InputCompany URL or a funding-news feed item
  2. ResearchSite pages, news search, filings; each fact tagged with its source and tier
  3. AI analysisLLM returns strict JSON: signals, fit, personas, risks, conflicts
  4. Structured outputValidated, scored with the formula above
  5. DatabaseSnapshot per run; the diff between snapshots feeds “What changed”
  6. DigestTop 5 pushed to email or Slack with one action each
